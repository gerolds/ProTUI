## 2026-11-05, after experiment 1 (bot runs and captures; the user had not played)

the dive should run automatically, with auto-rolls where the player can merely use timed steering windows, (cursor keys) and the event's should be easily learnable decisions they make based on the features we have described. if we pause for decisions the dive feels too much like a text-adventure, not an abstraction of an action game. the UI should also not perform premature abstraction narratively. we have repeatedly had this problem that the wirting just doesn't work because it ignores all principles of good writing. it seems that the docs skill has leaked into narrative writing and the guidance on narrative was ignored entirely. (../../Company annd ../../Runner/Runner have had this issue too). The text should not announce what the player is supposed to infer/learn/understand on their own. the UI should be a focus/select menu, not shortcuts. the whole app should assume a small vocabulary of actions that do conceptually similar things in every context. "tooltips" or "context" for the selected option is an opportunity to add flavour and narrative, explain what a thing is, this then lets us name a thing plainly in narrative terms and explain it diegetically without telepgraphing at maximum volume. Also mind that the fantasy is not campy 80s retro scifi setting but rather the world shown in ../Support/CLAIM7.wiki/CLAIM7/Alternative-Reality*. TENDER, HUSK, CUTTER, POD etc. are all fine, we dont have to overdo it, just mind that we're in a weird, peculiar world where distant authority, myth, local adaptation, frontier life, ignorant laws and a noble worker are themes.

the hook with the kernel + shells is generally right, shells should be something we unlock access to via tool/gear progression, underequipped we survive, but only briefly. the loop is a lot about digging, cutting, prying/plucking, shimmying, surviving the hostile environment, balancing pressures, dangerous  pockets, effects, events and moments of respite, with what we dig up, refining/purchasing consumables, extrating intermediate results etc. (latent metal gear solid 5 structure).
